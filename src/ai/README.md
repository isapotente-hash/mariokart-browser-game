# Opponents

`AiDriver.ts` follows sampled 3D course frames with speed-dependent look-ahead, curvature/braking-distance speed choices, surface and hazard response, lane overtaking cooldowns and seeded traits. Difficulty changes pace and reaction. Eleven AI compete with one human, or ten with two humans; Time Trial has none. AI uses the same controller, progression, inventory, damage and rescue interfaces as humans. Airborne behavior steers and pitches toward the landing route. No physics exemptions, progress shortcuts, rubber-banding or catch-up teleports are used. Stuck recovery pays the shared two-second checkpoint-rescue delay.
Arena battles use `ArenaDriver.ts`: available-supply seeking, reactive opponent tracking/prediction, cached cover visibility-graph navigation and spatial item/kart avoidance. Progress is geometric movement, never laps or checkpoint distance. A stuck kart reverses before requesting the shared battle rescue penalty; respawn clears recovery state. Difficulty uses the same seeded pace/reaction traits as racing. No opponent ignores arena collision, damage protection or inventory limits.
